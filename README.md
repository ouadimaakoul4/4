## 🌍 Vision

**"4"** stands for **For Humanity** — a massive open research vault containing
code, architectures, mathematical papers, theoretical works, and systems
designed to accelerate **safe AGI** and humanity’s transition into a
**multiplanetary species**.

This repository is a **living research hub**. It is not a single product, but a
collection of interconnected projects that span:

- Artificial General Intelligence (AGI)
- AI Safety, Alignment & Hardware Verification
- Advanced Mathematics & Theoretical Foundations
- Cognitive Architectures & Symbolic Reasoning
- Advanced Robotics & Bio-Robotics
- Mars Settlement & In-Situ Resource Utilization (ISRU)
- Space Technologies & Cislunar Logistics
- Ethical, Compassionate & Equitable Systems

##  Mars Living Systems Simulator (MLSS 4.1)

The **Mars Living Systems Simulator (MLSS 4.1)** is a fully browser‑based,
professional‑grade digital twin of a Martian colony, rigorously grounded in
**NASA‑STS‑7009B** and related standards.

> **Status:** Blueprint & Research Phase → See the [MLSS 4.1 Research-Backed
> Final Blueprint](https://github.com/ouadimaakoul4/4) (included in this repo).

### What It Does

- Simulates **O₂/CO₂ loops, water recovery, thermal management, and power
  grids** with real‑time mass‑energy balance.
- Models **human crew agents** with stress, trust, skill decay, and override
  behavior.
- Tracks **thermodynamic, cognitive, biological, and economic entropy** in a
  unified "Regret Ledger."
- Supports **counterfactual branching** — every human override is compared
  against AI‑recommended paths.
- Exports **SysML (XMI)** models and **PRISM** model‑checker files for formal
  verification.
- Runs entirely in the browser via **Rust → WebAssembly** + **React / Three.js**.
