
# 4 Humanity 

**An open-source hub for Artificial General Intelligence, Advanced Robotics, Space Exploration, and Multiplanetary Civilization.**

Open knowledge. Open future. 

---

## Vision

**"4"** stands for **For Humanity** — a massive open research vault containing code, architectures, experiments, **mathematical papers**, and systems built to accelerate **safe AGI** and humanity’s multiplanetary future.

### Core Focus Areas
- Artificial General Intelligence (AGI)
- AI Safety, Alignment & Hardware Verification
- **Advanced Mathematics & Theoretical Foundations**
- Cognitive Architectures & Symbolic Reasoning
- Advanced Robotics & Bio-Robotics
- Mars Settlement & In-Situ Resource Utilization (ISRU)
- Space Technologies & Multiplanetary Infrastructure
- Ethical & Compassionate AI
